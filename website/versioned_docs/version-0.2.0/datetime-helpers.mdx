# DateTime helpers

Extension getters and methods on `DateTime`: calendar facts, the start and end of any unit, DST-safe arithmetic, differences and comparisons.

## Properties

<PropertiesDemo />

`weekOfYear` is the ISO-8601 week number (weeks start on Monday, week 1 holds the first Thursday). `dayOfWeek` and `startOfWeek` follow the locale's first day of the week.

## Arithmetic

Days, weeks, months and years are calendar steps: the wall-clock time survives daylight saving time changes, and January 31 plus one month is February 28.

<ArithmeticDemo />

## Differences and comparisons

`diff` in days and weeks counts calendar days across DST. Comparisons accept a `unit` granularity: `isSame(other, unit: Unit.month)` is true for any two dates in the same month.
